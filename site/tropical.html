Tropical atmospheric waves — MJO, Kelvin, equatorial Rossby, MRG (BoM)

—frame 0 / 0 · dashed marker = today

The Global Tropics Hazards Outlook is CPC's week 2–3 view of where tropical cyclone formation and heavy rain are favoured. The MJO diagrams show which phase the Madden–Julian Oscillation is in and where it's heading — phases 1–2 (and 8) tend to favour Atlantic development, 4–6 suppress it. All images are served live from NOAA CPC and the Australian Bureau of Meteorology.

Track and intensity guidance is drawn from the NHC ATCF a-deck, the same guidance file NHC forecasters use. Model availability varies by storm and cycle; a model shown dashed came from an earlier cycle than the newest one available. This is model output, not a forecast — for official forecasts and warnings see the National Hurricane Center.
